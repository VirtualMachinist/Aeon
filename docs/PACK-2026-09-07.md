# Sprite packer and selector table — 2026-09-07

Fable.Halo, after the v0.1 review. Sim and art untouched; the client loads
the same pictures faster and smaller, and chooses them from one table.

## What changed

**Packer (`aeon --pack`).** Loads both bodies the slow way once, asks each
set for every cell it can resolve, crops each unique source rectangle to its
alpha bounds, shelf-packs the crops into 4096-wide pages, and writes
`crates/client/assets/packed/<body>-<n>.png` plus `<body>.manifest`
(`cell page x y w h anchor_x anchor_y height` per line). Anchors and heights
are re-expressed for the crop (`sprites::refit`), so feet and drawn size are
unchanged. Partial pages are stored no taller than they need. The directory
is git-ignored; `Play-Aeon.command` packs when the manifest is missing.

**Loader.** `SpriteSet::load` reads the manifest and pages when they exist
and falls back to the sixty-odd sheets when they do not. Pages decode two at
a time on worker threads. Every cell resolves through `frame()` exactly as
before; the pose fallback chain (walk → idle, etc.) still applies.

**Selector table.** `cell_for` is now a loop over `sprites::SELECTORS`: each
entry names its pick function and the cells that must be present before its
family is used, so a partly missing family never mixes authored and fallback
pictures inside one move. The previous chain of thirty guarded `if`s is gone.

**Style table.** `Cell::style(body)` returns rigid / pinned / same-cell trail
/ short trail / cut flags from one table (`sprites::STYLES`, one row per
family, Kogan and Raya columns). `anim.rs` reads the flags; the three
hand-maintained allowlists it carried are gone. Rules are unchanged and
tested (`style_table_reproduces_the_reviewed_rules`).

## Measured on castle (Apple Silicon, release, `--smoke`)

| | Sheets (before) | Packed (after) |
|---|---|---|
| Files loaded | 143 PNGs | 5 pages + 2 manifests |
| Asset load time | 1,388 ms | 536 ms |
| Settled resident memory | 748 MB | 422 MB |
| Peak resident memory | 797 MB | 871 MB (two pages decoding) |
| On disk | 167 MB | 113 MB (generated) |

Cells: Kogan 208 (198 unique crops from 72 sheets, 3 pages), Raya 213
(206 unique crops from 67 sheets, 2 pages). Pixels: 571 MB of source sheets
become 253 MB of crops.

The `--polish-preview --capture --capture-1x` trace is byte-identical in
both modes. Six sampled frames (run trail, rekka contact, launch, floor,
Raya ascension, convergence) differ on 0.3–0.6% of pixels by at most 12
summed RGB levels: filter edges of the cropped rectangles, invisible.

## Tests

191 (89 sim + 102 client). New: packer alpha bounds, refit, shelf placement
without overlap, manifest round trip, style table rules, cell key round
trip. `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings` clean.

## Limits and next

- Peak memory during load is transient and could be cut by decoding pages
  one at a time (about +200 ms). Steady state is what play sees.
- Pose pictures are 800×800 and the animation cells are roughly half that
  body height; the packed pages carry both at source resolution. Packing at
  a single body resolution would roughly halve the pages again. That is an
  art-quality decision, not taken here.
- The review harness (`kit_preview` and the preview modules) still compiles
  into the play binary. A `review` feature is the next size cut.
