# Aeon — motion pass

Follows the [first polish pass](POLISH-2026-09-05.md). Direction from Evan on 2026-09-05: stick tuning waits until the animation is mature, so develop each character fully, with creative freedom inside the spirit of the mechanics and the aesthetic. No image generation was available in this session; the pass builds the motion system the art was always meant to sit in, makes every existing picture move, and fixes two simulation truths the motion exposed.

## Simulation

- **A launched body rides the arc.** Air hitstun now ends on the floor, never in the air. Before this pass the defender's hitstun expired mid-launch, they became a free jump, landed in two frames and were standing before the uppercut's own landing tax ended: the uppercut was punishable on hit and never knocked down. Now `Hit` persists until touchdown; with `knockdown` set it becomes the hard knockdown, otherwise it lands like a full jump. The combo counter holds through the arc, so uppercut xx RC juggles scale instead of restarting. Tests: `a_launched_body_rides_the_arc_into_the_hard_knockdown` (both bodies), `a_juggled_body_whose_stun_ended_in_the_air_lands_like_a_jump`.
- **Corner pushback transfers to the attacker.** A cornered defender's lost shove now moves the attacker instead, as in Super Turbo, so corner pressure still costs spacing. Shots do not shove their owner. Test: `cornered_pushback_moves_the_attacker_but_shots_do_not`. Documented in the generated universal table.

No move numbers, cancel windows, gauge costs or inputs changed. The sim still has zero dependencies and no floats.

## Client

- **Motion layer** (`crates/client/src/anim.rs`). Every state of both kits is a function of simulation state: anticipation settles back, contact drives forward with a stretch and a two-pixel push, recovery eases home. Prejump squashes, the rise stretches, landings squash. The run leans and streams copper afterimages; Raya's processional turns her to written light for its duration. Launched bodies tumble and land flat into the lying pose; the floor bounce follows. Hurt and block lean away by remaining stun. Feints flicker. Consecrate hums. The revolver kicks after the shot leaves. A changed picture crossfades over two frames (except on floor contact and air hits, which cut). During hitstop the struck body shudders and the striker leans on the hit. All of it is deterministic from `World` plus a twelve-frame drawn history, so pause, frame-step, hitstop and replays hold.
- **Impact layer** (`crates/client/src/fx.rs`). Hit sparks in the attacker's matter (copper rays and a cyan hexagon for Kogan, cyan rays and linen for Raya; heavy hits add the cut streak), block sparks, throw impact, tech ring, floor dust on knockdown and full-jump landings, Roman Cancel ring and shards, feint sparkles, EX and super rings, revolver muzzle flash, glyph and crystal casts, disc-guard ring, shot clash, armed pulse, crystal blast, KO ring. Bodies flash white on a clean hit, cyan on an EX start, white with a cyan ring on a super, whose start also dims the vault for fourteen frames. Effects live in simulation frames, so hitstop holds a spark on the point of contact. No camera work.
- **Flash shader** (`render.rs::Flash`). A small GLSL material mixes a sprite toward a colour before tinting, used for hit flashes and afterimages. If it fails to compile on a box, draws fall back to a tint.
- **Win pose.** The round winner shows the previously unused win picture from thirty frames into the round-end and on the match-over screen.
- **Review.** `--polish-preview` gains a reaction scene per body: uppercut launch and knockdown, command grab, super. The preview is now eight scenes, 2,100 ticks, 1,050 captured frames at 30 fps. Its test asserts each body launches, grabs once and lands the super.
