7. Brake and recover: torso upright above hips, both knees softly bent, feet settling into familiar ready spacing, one palm quietly opening forward near waist and other relaxing beside ribs. The body receives travel through bent knees, cloak catches up behind. This supported braking gesture can recover from either glide direction.

Never scale low poses up or make forward glide a diagonal flying body. Every silhouette stays completely inside its own cell, separated from neighbors by uninterrupted vertical green gutters. No ground, cast shadows, scenery, grids, borders, labels, text, trails, blur, sparks, effects, weapons or extra characters. Render only the complete eight-key green sheet.
